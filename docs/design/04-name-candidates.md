# Name Candidates

The name is chosen from the story. Each candidate is scored 1–5 on four criteria:

- **Story fit**: does it capture the game's heart (late truth, a drowned world, a courier)?
- **Hook**: would a stranger scrolling itch.io click it?
- **Searchability**: can people find it on Google and itch.io, or is it buried under other results?
- **Uniqueness**: no existing game with the same or a confusingly similar name.

| Name | Meaning | Story fit | Hook | Search | Unique | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| ~~Dead Letter~~ | Presumed dead + an undelivered letter | 5 | 4 | 1 | **1** | ✗ |

\* Not yet checked thoroughly. Search Steam, itch.io and Google before committing.

**Checked so far (30 Oct 2026):**
- **Dead Letter** is out: *Dead Letter Dept.* (Belief Engine, Steam, 2025) is a well-known,
  highly rated game with a mail theme, and itch.io already has several "dead letter" games.
- **Hollowmere**: no existing game found.

## Analysis

- **Floodword** scores highest: it's unique, easy to search, and the invented word means the name
  is yours. Its weakness is that it explains nothing at first glance, so a subtitle helps:
  *Floodword: A Story Carried by Hand*.
- **One Day Late** is the most emotional, and players will understand it fully once they reach
  Act II, which is a strong "oh, that's what the title meant" moment. It's harder to search.
- **Hollowmere** sounds like a classic fantasy title and is easy to search, but it's about a place,
  not the player's story.

**A common pattern worth considering**: an invented word as the title plus an emotional subtitle,
for example **Floodword: One Day Late**. That gets the search benefits of the invented word and
the emotional hook in one.

## Decision

**Chosen (30 Oct 2026): *Floodword: One Day Late*.**

- **Floodword**, the invented word, is the searchable brand: it's what the itch.io page, URL and logo use.
- **One Day Late**, the subtitle, carries the emotional hook, and its meaning lands in Act II.
- **Conflict check:** no existing game called "Floodword" was found. The closest is *Flood World*,
  a small sandbox game on itch.io. It sounds similar but is spelled differently, and the subtitle
  separates them.

**Availability (checked 30 Oct 2026):** `floodword.itch.io` returns 404, so no account uses it, and
`floodword.com` has no record in the .com registry (RDAP 404). Both appear free. Register them before
announcing the name, because availability can change.
